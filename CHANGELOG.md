# Changelog

## 2.0.0 (November 2026)

A ground-up rebuild.

- **Two futures, honestly.** Forecasts now come from the Future Engine, a Monte Carlo simulation of your own follow-through, shown as a range. The made-up "confidence score" from v1 is gone.
- **Commitments and reality checks.** Commit to a move for 90 days, log with one tap, and compare the forecast with what happened on day 7, 30 and 90.
- **Calibration.** See how your stated confidence compares with what you actually do.
- **Letters to future you**, sealed until the day you choose.
- **Guest mode.** Try everything without an account; your data moves over when you sign up.
- **New design.** "Ink & Ember" colour system (three hues, WCAG-checked, Day and Night), new Y-fork mark, editorial type and 3D motion throughout.
- **Wisdom, fact-checked.** Every quote traced to its source; misattributions corrected or replaced.
- **Security.** AI calls moved to a server-side gateway (no keys in the browser), per-plan quotas, stricter database rules.
- **Safety.** Crisis language now surfaces helplines instead of coaching.

## 1.0.0 (April 2026)

First release: mood and mode check-in, AI suggestions with 7/30/90-day outcomes, history and a quote feed. MIT licensed (see repository history).
