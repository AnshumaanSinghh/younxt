Questions
Is this therapy?
No. YouNxt is a self-improvement tool for everyday decisions and habits. If you are struggling, please talk to a professional. The app shows crisis lines if it detects you might be in danger.
Where do the numbers come from?
A Monte Carlo simulation of your commitment. It starts from a cautious prior and updates with every session you log, done or skipped. AI writes the words; the numbers never come from an AI.
Which AI do you use?
Signed-in users get moves written by Google Gemini or open models on Groq, called from our server so no keys ever reach your browser. If AI is unavailable, the on-device engine answers instantly.
What happens to my data?
Guest data stays on your device. Account data lives in your private Firestore space that only you can read. Export it as JSON or delete your account at any time from the You tab.
